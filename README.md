Your debts, expenses, and financial records — all in one place.

Debt Notes is a clean, minimal, and completely offline personal notebook designed to help you keep track of money you need to receive, money you need to pay, and your everyday expenses without clutter or complicated spreadsheets.

Whether you are splitting bills with friends, lending money to a colleague, tracking shop credit, or logging daily household expenses, Debt Notes keeps every record organized and accessible right on your phone.

**KEY FEATURES**

• Track Debts (To Receive & To Pay)
Easily record who owes you ("They need to pay me") and whom you owe ("I need to pay them"). View your net outstanding balance, total receivable, and total payable at a glance on the Home screen.

• People Directory & Balances
Automatically groups your debt notes by person so you can see your net balance and active note count with each individual in one clean list.

• Daily Expense & Income Notes
Create itemized expense sheets with multiple entries. Mark individual line items as Income (+) or Expense (−) and let Debt Notes calculate the net total automatically.

• Proof Image Attachments
Attach payment screenshots, bills, or receipts directly from your Camera or Gallery to any debt or expense note. Tap any attached image to inspect it in full view.

• Reliable Alarm Reminders
Set custom date and time reminders—or use quick presets (+1 min, +1 hr, Tomorrow 9 AM)—to get notified with sound and vibration when it is time to collect or settle a payment.

• Quick Search, Filters & Undo
Search notes by name or keyword, filter by All, Receive, Pay, or Paid, mark settled notes as paid with a single tap, and undo accidental deletions instantly.

• 100% Offline & Private
Your financial records belong only to you. Debt Notes works completely offline, requires no sign-up or internet connection, contains no ads, and stores all notes and proof photos locally on your device.

• Easy JSON Backup & Restore
Safeguard your records or move them to a new phone anytime by exporting, importing, or sharing a portable JSON backup file from Settings.

• Multilingual & Personalized Interface
Use the app in English, Hindi (हिंदी), or Telugu (తెలుగు). Switch effortlessly between Light Mode and Dark Mode with 6 clean accent colors (Ocean, Emerald, Amber, Rose, Violet, and Cyan).

**IMPORTANT NOTE**
Debt Notes is a personal offline record-keeping and note-taking utility. It does not provide loans, credit lines, banking services, or payment processing.
